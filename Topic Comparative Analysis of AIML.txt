Title :AI/ML-Based Landslide Risk Assessment Using Environmental and Geospatial Data

Objectives--

1) To study landslide risk factors in the North Eastern Region of India using historical landslide records, rainfall, terrain, vegetation, and other available environmental data.

2) To collect and integrate relevant datasets from sources such as the NASA Global Landslide Catalog, Indian rainfall datasets, SRTM terrain data, and Sentinel-2 satellite-derived vegetation information.

3) To preprocess and prepare the dataset for machine learning, including handling missing values, feature extraction, normalization, and selection of relevant features.
To develop and train multiple AI/ML models for landslide risk assessment using the prepared dataset.
